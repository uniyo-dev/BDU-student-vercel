TIP: Always rank your MOST preferred department as #1. The system tries your 1st choice first, then 2nd, and so on.
How Placement Algorithm Works
Step 1: System evaluates your 1st choice first
Step 2: If 1st choice has capacity AND your score qualifies → SELECTED
Step 3: If 1st choice is full or you don't qualify → Move to 2nd choice
Step 4: Continue until selected or all choices exhausted

Example:
1st choice: Other Social Sciences → SELECTED (stop here)
2nd choice: Law → Not evaluated (not needed)

The system ONLY moves to your next choice if your current choice fails.
Important Rules
  • CGPA is the dominant factor (50% weight)
  • Program Entrance Exam = 30% (only for Law, Medicine, etc.)
  • University Entrance Exam = 20%
  • Higher CGPA = better chance of 1st choice
  • Each department has capacity limits
  • System stops at your FIRST successful choice
  • Once deadline passes, no changes allowed
  • Take a screenshot of your confirmation
