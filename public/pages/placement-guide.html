📊 Placement Criteria

Your placement score is calculated based on the following criteria:

Criteria Weight
University Current CGPA 50%
Program Entrance Exam 30%
University Entrance Exam 20%
Gender (Female) +5% Bonus
Gender Emerging Region (F) +5% Bonus
Handicapped +5% Bonus
Handicapped Emerging Region (F) +5% Bonus
📝 How to Select Departments
1
Login to Portal
Use your BDU username and password
2
Go to "Department Placement"
Find it in the navigation menu
3
Rank Your Choices
Select departments in order of preference (1st, 2nd, 3rd...)
4
Save & Submit
Review carefully before final submission
5
Check Result
After Meskerem 5-8, login to see your assigned department

⚠️ Important Rules
  • CGPA is the dominant factor (50% weight)
  • Higher CGPA = better chance of first choice
  • Each department has capacity limits
  • Once deadline passes, no changes allowed
  • Take a screenshot of your confirmation
📍 Campus Locations
🏛️ Peda Campus (Belay Zeleke) - Central:
• College of Business & Economics (All BA programs)
• College of Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Math, Statistics, Biotechnology, Geology)
• College of Humanities (English, Amharic, History, Geography, Sociology, Social Work, PSIR, Journalism, Civics)
• College of Education (Psychology, EdPM, Special Needs, Adult Education, ECCE)
• Academy of Sport (Sport Science, Football Coaching)

🏛️ Yibab Campus - Southwestern:
• Governance and Development Studies
• School of Law (LLB)
• Institute of Land Administration (Land Admin, Geomatics, Real Estate)
• Disaster Risk Management (DRM, Food Security)

🏛️ Poly Campus (BiT) - Nile River Outlet:
• All Engineering (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Industrial)
• Software Engineering, Computer Science, IT
• Water Resources, Hydraulics, Mechatronics, Automotive, Food Engineering

🏛️ EiTEX Campus - Adjacent to Poly:
• Textile Engineering, Garment Engineering
• Fashion Design, Leather Engineering

🏛️ Tibebe Ghion Campus - Health Sciences:
• Medicine (MD), Pharmacy, Anesthesia, Optometry
• Medical Lab, Nursing, Midwifery, Psychiatry Nursing
• Pediatric Nursing, Surgical Nursing, Public Health
• Medical Radiology, Environmental Health

🏛️ Zenzelma Campus - Eastern Outskirts:
• Plant Sciences, Animal Sciences, Horticulture
• Natural Resources, Soil Management, Forestry
• Agricultural Economics, Rural Development, Fisheries & Wildlife
