Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about BD Buddy

🚀 Getting Started

What is BD Buddy?
BD Buddy is an unofficial, student-built viewer for the Bahir Dar University student portal. It fetches your real academic data from studentportal.bdu.edu.et and presents it in a clean, fast, mobile-friendly interface.
How do I log in?
Use your official BDU credentials — the same username and password you use on studentportal.bdu.edu.et. No separate registration is required.
Is BD Buddy free?
Yes, completely free. No ads, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. Built by a student for students.
Can I install it as an app?
Yes! BD Buddy is a PWA (Progressive Web App).
  • Android/Chrome: Tap the menu → "Add to Home screen"
  • iOS/Safari: Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"

🔒 Security & Privacy

Is my password safe?
Yes. Your password is forwarded directly to BDU's official portal for authentication. It is never stored on our servers, never logged, and never shared with anyone.
Do you store my grades?
No. BD Buddy fetches your data on-demand and displays it in your browser. Nothing is cached on our servers, and your session data stays on your device.
Can BDU see that I'm using BD Buddy?
BDU sees the same login attempt that it would see if you used the official portal directly. BD Buddy does not bypass any security — it uses the same authentication system.
Is BD Buddy affiliated with BDU?
No. BD Buddy is an unofficial, student-built tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Bahir Dar University.

📊 Using BD Buddy

Where is my GPA?
After login, you'll see your CGPA and semester GPA on the main dashboard. A detailed grade breakdown (A+, A, B+, B, etc.) is also shown.
How do I see my courses?
Go to the Dashboard → your registered courses are listed by semester, with course codes, titles, credit hours, and letter grades.
Where are my placement results?
Click Placement in the navigation. You'll see your department placement, priority ranking, and score breakdown.
Can I download my transcript?
A grade report viewer is available. Full transcript PDF export is coming soon in a future update.

🔧 Troubleshooting

💡 Tip: Before reporting a bug, check if the same issue exists on the official portal at studentportal.bdu.edu.et. If yes, it's a BDU issue.
Login isn't working. What should I do?
Try this checklist:
  • Check your username and password on studentportal.bdu.edu.et first
  • Make sure you have an active internet connection
  • Clear your browser cache and try again
  • Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
  • Wait 5 minutes — BDU's portal may be under heavy load
My data looks wrong or outdated
BD Buddy fetches data live from BDU's portal. If it's wrong, BDU's portal is likely wrong too. Always trust the official portal if there's any discrepancy. Report the issue to BDU's registrar.
The page won't load / blank screen
Try:
  • Refresh the page (pull down to refresh on mobile)
  • Clear your browser cache
  • Check your internet connection
  • Close and reopen the app
If it still fails, the server may be restarting. Wait 1-2 minutes and try again.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Before reporting:
  • Check if it also happens at studentportal.bdu.edu.et
  • Note what you were doing when it happened
  • Take a screenshot if possible
Then send it through the contact link below.
