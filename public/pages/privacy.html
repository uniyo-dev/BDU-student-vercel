Privacy Policy

How BD Buddy handles your data

Last updated: January 2025

The Short Version (TL;DR)

  • We never store your password
  • We never cache your academic data
  • We never share your information with anyone
  • Your credentials go directly to BDU's official portal
  • We don't use analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind
1 What BD Buddy Is

BD Buddy is an unofficial, student-built viewer for the Bahir Dar University student portal at studentportal.bdu.edu.et.

It is not affiliated with Bahir Dar University, and it is not an official BDU service. It simply retrieves your real academic data from BDU's portal and displays it in a cleaner interface.

2 Information We Collect

BD Buddy collects only what's strictly necessary to authenticate you with BDU's portal:

Data Type Collected? Purpose
BDU Username Yes (temporary) Sent to BDU to log you in
BDU Password Yes (in-memory only) Sent to BDU to log you in
Academic Data Yes (displayed only) Shown in your browser
Session Cookie Yes (local) Keeps you logged in during your visit
Email / Phone No —
Location Data No —
Device Fingerprint No —
Analytics / Tracking No —
3 How We Handle Your Password
Your BDU password is used only to authenticate with BDU's official portal. It is never stored, logged, or shared.

Here's exactly what happens when you log in:

  • You type your username and password into BD Buddy
  • BD Buddy sends them directly to studentportal.bdu.edu.et
  • BDU verifies your credentials and returns your data
  • BD Buddy displays the data in your browser
  • Your password is never saved to disk
  • Your password is never sent anywhere else
  • Your password is never visible to the developer
4 Data Storage

Server side: BD Buddy does not store any student data on its servers. Each request is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the response is sent.

Client side (your browser): The following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • A session cookie (to keep you logged in during your visit)
  • Your theme preference (light/dark mode) — if applicable
  • Your language preference — if applicable

These are stored only on your device. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

5 Third-Party Services

BD Buddy uses minimal third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — For typography (no personal data shared)
  • Render.com — For hosting (see their Privacy Policy)
  • No analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, etc.)
  • No ads (no AdSense, no ad networks)
  • No tracking pixels
  • No social media pixels
6 Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Log out at any time to clear your session
  • Clear browser data to remove all local storage
  • Use BD Buddy anonymously — no registration required
  • Stop using BD Buddy at any time
7 Security

BD Buddy uses HTTPS encryption for all connections. Your login credentials and academic data are encrypted in transit between your browser, BD Buddy, and BDU's portal.

Important: No system is 100% secure. If you have concerns about your BDU account security, contact BDU's IT department directly.
8 Children's Privacy

BD Buddy is designed for university students. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 16. If you are under 16, please use the official BDU portal instead.

9 Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Continued use of BD Buddy after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10 Contact

  • Report issues via the developer contact on the About page
