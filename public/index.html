📖 What is BD Buddy?

An unofficial, student-built web app that shows your BDU academic data in a cleaner, faster, mobile-friendly way.

✨ What it does

  • GPA & grade breakdown
  • Course registration details
  • Placement results
  • Student profile
  • PWA (installable on phone)

🔒 Your security

  • Credentials sent directly to BDU
  • Password never stored
  • Data never cached on server
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics

❌ BD Buddy does NOT

  • Store your password
  • Share data with third parties
  • Pose as the official BDU portal
